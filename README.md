AI-Powered Interactive Medicinal Plant Heritage Explorer Background Traditional medicinal knowledge represents an important part of India's cultural and scientific heritage. The provided Medicinal Plant Information (MPI) dataset contains structured information about medicinal plants, including their botanical identity, regional names, morphological descriptions, medicinal uses, plant parts, active constituents, pharmacological properties, Siddha-related information, and available research evidence. However, this information is currently available primarily as a structured dataset, which can be difficult for students, researchers, traditional-knowledge enthusiasts, and the general public to explore interactively. The challenge is to transform this dataset into an interactive, engaging, and gamified digital medicinal-plant exploration platform. Challenge Develop an interactive Medicinal Plant Heritage Explorer that enables users to discover, identify, learn about, and interact with medicinal plants using the information provided in the MPI dataset. The application should go beyond a conventional search or database interface. Participants are encouraged to develop a learning and exploration experience in which users can discover plants, solve challenges, identify plant species, learn about their traditional uses. Mandatory Requirements • The IEEE MPI dataset must be a core and demonstrable component of the final solution. • Teams must be able to show where the dataset enters the system and how it influences the final AI capability. • A generic chatbot with the dataset merely attached as background material will not satisfy the intended challenge. • Teams should provide an appropriate grounding, retrieval, evidence or evaluation mechanism for generated outputs where applicable. Creative Freedom Teams may choose generative AI, RAG, knowledge graphs, multimodal systems, classification, recommendation, semantic search, AI agents or hybrid architectures. The product concept, model, interface and user experience are open.
